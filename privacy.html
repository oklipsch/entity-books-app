Privacy Policy

Entity Books. Last updated: November 14, 2026.

Who this is for

Entity Books is an internal tool used only by AWK Holdings, Inc.; Maley & Wertz, Inc.; Klipsch's Coffee, LLC; and the persons they authorize. It is not offered to the public and has no outside customers.

What data it accesses

With the company's explicit authorization, the tool reads that company's QuickBooks Online accounting data: company information, chart of accounts, financial reports, journal entries, bills, purchases, deposits and vendors. Access is read-only. Customer and employee names are not returned by the tool.

How it is stored

The QuickBooks connection tokens are kept in a private database with no public access, used only by the tool's server. Tokens are never shown to users. The tool keeps a short log of each request (time, company, type of request, success) and does not keep copies of accounting data on its server. Reports pulled for bookkeeping may be saved by the company's own staff on company devices.

How it is used

Data is used only for the companies' own bookkeeping, reporting and review, including analysis with an AI assistant (Anthropic's Claude) working on the owner's behalf. Data is never sold, rented or used for advertising.

Your choices

A company can disconnect the tool at any time in QuickBooks Online (Settings, Apps). After disconnecting, no further data is read.
